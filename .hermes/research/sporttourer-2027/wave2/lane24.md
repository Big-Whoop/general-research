## 3. Reliability / recalls / TSBs (axis 3)

**F3.1 — Official US recall list: ONLY the 2016 GSX-S1000 A/FA front brake caliper recall for the GSX-S1000 platform (suzukicycles.com).**
- Claim: The official Suzuki Motor USA recalls index (refreshed Oct 15, 2026) lists exactly ONE recall for the GSX-S1000 platform: the "2016 GSX-S1000 A FA Front Brake Caliper Recall Notice" (campaign 2A48). There are NO MY2022-2027 GSX-S1000/GSX-S1000GT recalls on the official list.
- Source: https://suzukicycles.com/recalls (official manufacturer recalls index, refreshed Oct 15, 2026).
- Quote: "2016 GSX-S1000 A FA Front Brake Caliper Recall Notice" (the only GSX-S1000 entry; PDF: safety-recall-campaign-2a48-gsxs1000-front-brake-caliper-customer-letter.pdf).
- Tier: **Verified** (official, read).

**F3.2 — Hayabusa brake recalls are a DIFFERENT model (do not conflate) (suzukicycles.com).**
- Claim: The repeated front-brake-master-cylinder recalls on the official list are for the Hayabusa (GSX1300R), 2022-2024 (campaign 3A04) and 2022 — NOT the GSX-S1000/GSX-S1000GT.
- Source: same suzukicycles.com/recalls.
- Quote: "2022 to 2024 Hayabusa Front Brake Master Cylinder Reservoir Cap Diaphragm Safety Recall" / "2022 Hayabusa Front Brake Master Cylinder Safety Recall Notice."
- Tier: **Verified** (official, read).

**F3.3 — TSB: engine calibration software update (NHTSA, Aug 2023).**
- Claim: A 2023 GSX-S1000 TSB (Bulletin 016401, posted Aug 7, 2023) covers "Calibration software updates, various models" (engine and engine cooling) — a minor software calibration, not a safety recall.
- Source: https://suzukiforum.com/forum/tsb_recall/tsbs/suzuki/gsx-s1000/2023 (NHTSA TSB data via suzukiforum.com).
- Quote: "Bulletin Number: 016401 / Date Posted: Aug 7, 2023 / Component Name: ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING / Summary: Calibration software updates, various models."
- Tier: **Verified** (read; TSB, not a recall).

**F3.4 — TSB: brake lamp switch adjustment and cruise control function (NHTSA, Dec 2022).**
- Claim: A 2023 GSX-S1000 TSB (Bulletin M-T-0049, posted Dec 21, 2022) covers "Brake lamp switch adjustment and cruise control function" (electrical system) — relevant to the owner-reported cruise-control glitch (F2.9).
- Source: same suzukiforum.com TSB page.
- Quote: "Bulletin Number: M-T-0049 / Date Posted: Dec 21, 2022 / Component Name: ELECTRICAL SYSTEM / Summary: Brake lamp switch adjustment and cruise control function."
- Tier: **Verified** (read; TSB, not a recall).
