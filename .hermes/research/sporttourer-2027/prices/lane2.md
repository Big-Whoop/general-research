# LANE-2 — Swiss prices (CHF) + dealer/importer availability

**Lane:** RESEARCH-sporttourer-2027 [LANE-2]
**Scope:** For each MY2027 sport-tourer candidate: official Swiss list price in CHF **incl. 8.1% VAT** (where available; noted if ex-VAT / range / dealer-only), and Swiss importer/dealer availability (which importer, which variants on sale, lead-time signals).
**Market:** Switzerland. **Model year:** MY2027 = bikes on sale in CH in late 2026 / 2027.
**Date of research:** 2026-10-17.
**Tier key:** **Verified** = read directly off the official importer page/price list (authoritative for this exact target). **Plausible** = dealer/aggregator figure corroborating, or official price not yet re-confirmed for MY2027. **Speculative** = MY2027 figure not yet published / model not confirmed in CH lineup.

> **VAT note (applies to all "official" prices below):** Every official Swiss importer price captured here is explicitly stated as *inkl. 8.1% MwSt.* / *incl. 8.1% VAT* (unverbindliche Preisempfehlung des Herstellers für die Schweiz). BMW, Yamaha, Triumph, Aprilia, Ducati, Kawasaki, Honda and the Suzuki 2026 price list all carry this wording. Dealer prices (MotoTech, Honda Zürich, 1000ps.ch, mototrader) are likewise VAT-inclusive Swiss retail.

## Gaps / open items for the collector

1. **MY2027 re-pricing not yet published for several models.** The CH official pages currently carry MY2025/MY2026 prices (BMW S 1000 XR "2026 NEW", Yamaha Tracer 7 "2024" PDP, Triumph "2025", Aprilia Factory "2026"). Where a manufacturer has not published a distinct MY2027 CH price, the current figure is the best available and the MY2027 delta is **Speculative**. The R 1300 RS is already labeled "2027" (so its 16'700–16'800 is the MY2027 price).
2. **KTM current official CH list price** — ktm.com CH page 404'd; only a 2024 new-vehicle figure (16'990) was obtainable. Recommend the collector re-probe ktm.com (or a KTM CH dealer) for the live MY2026/MY2027 1290 Super Duke R / RR price.
3. **Ducati Hyperstrada & Yamaha FJ-09** — not confirmed in the CH lineup; recommend a dealer confirmation before including them in the final comparison (likely drop both from the MY2027 CH set).
4. **BMW R 1300 RS price discrepancy** — overview 16'700 vs detail 16'800 (CHF 100); confirm with a dealer which is the current base.
5. **Lead times** — no importer published explicit MY2027 lead times on the pages read; "ab CHF …" prices are list prices and dealer stock/lead time is per-dealer (not captured here). The Kawasaki CH site was intermittently 404-ing during this run (site under maintenance), which may also signal transition-period stock.
6. **Category flag for the final report** — Speed Triple 1200 RS, Tuono V4, 1290 Super Duke R, Hayabusa, CBR650R/CBR1000RR-R are naked/supersport/hyper-naked, **not** fairing sport-tourers. The "true" fairing sport-tourers in this set are: BMW S 1000 XR / M 1000 XR, BMW R 1300 RS, BMW F 900 XR, Kawasaki Ninja 1100SX SE / H2 SX SE, Suzuki GSX-S1000GT, Triumph Tiger Sport 800, Ducati Multistrada V4 family, Yamaha Tracer 7.
